Simple ordering process

How customers buy from us

Make it easy for customers to explain what they need. You handle design, material selection, production and delivery.

01

Customer sends a requirement

They share a photo, sketch, dimensions, CAD file, quantity and intended use.

02

We check and quote

Confirm feasibility, design effort, material, finish, lead time and price.

03

Approve a sample or prototype

For fit-sensitive jobs, print a test piece and get customer approval before batch production.

04

Print, inspect and deliver

Check dimensions and finish, pack the order and retain approved job specifications for reorders.

How we find customers

Visit industrial estates and repair clusters. Speak to store owners and maintenance managers. Ask what plastic part, fixture or display they struggle to source. Offer a sample only after understanding the use case.

How we win repeat orders

Save approved CAD files and dimensions, assign a part number, record material and revision, and offer reorder pricing. Build a small digital catalogue from real customer requests.
